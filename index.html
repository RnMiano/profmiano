Frequently Asked
Questions

Everything you need to know before we get started.

Is this 100% confidential? 🔒
Absolutely. I never share, publish, or discuss any student's work or identity with anyone. All communication and deliverables are kept completely private. Your secret is safe with me.
How fast can you deliver? ⚡
For short assignments, I can deliver within a few hours. For longer work like dissertations, timelines are discussed upfront. I always agree on a deadline before starting and I've never missed one.
What's included in the free Turnitin check? 📄
Every assignment I complete for you includes a free Turnitin Similarity Report covering both plagiarism similarity and AI writing detection. This is the official Turnitin report — not a third-party imitation tool.
Why does the external Turnitin check cost $10? 💳
Running a Turnitin check requires institutional access which has a cost. For work I've personally written, I absorb this cost as part of my service. For external documents I haven't written, the $10 fee covers the access cost and my time to process and deliver the official report.
What if I'm not happy with the work? 🔁
I offer unlimited free revisions until you're 100% satisfied. Simply send me specific feedback on what needs to change and I'll fix it promptly. My goal is your satisfaction and your grade.
Do you handle urgent, last-minute work? 🚨
Yes — I regularly handle urgent and same-day assignments. Just be upfront about your deadline and I'll tell you honestly whether I can deliver in time. Rush orders may carry a small urgency fee depending on complexity.
Can you match my writing style? ✍️
Yes. If you provide a sample of your previous work or describe your usual writing style, I can match it closely so the assignment sounds authentically like you. This is especially useful for continuous assessments and dissertations.
How do I pay? 💰
Payment methods are discussed directly and we find a method that works for both of us. I typically ask for a 50% deposit before starting and the remainder upon delivery. All transactions are secure and private.
